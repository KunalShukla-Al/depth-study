Which ball is closer? A short study on depth perception

This study compares how people and AI vision systems judge which of two objects is closer. You'll see pictures of two balls and choose the one that looks closer to you. It takes about 5–6 minutes, on a phone or a laptop.

  • Anonymous. No name, email or account. You get a random code; nothing links it to you.
  • What's recorded: your answers, how long each took, your screen size, and your answers to two screening questions (that you're 18 or over, and whether you have normal or corrected vision).
  • Voluntary. You can stop at any time by closing the page. Nothing is sent unless you reach the end.
  • Use: combined, anonymous results may be published in a research paper, and the anonymous data may be shared with other researchers.
  • There are no known risks beyond ordinary screen use. Take a break whenever you like.

Researcher: Kunal Shukla, B.Tech CSE (Data Science), SRM Institute of Science and Technology, Kattankulathur (ks6081@srmist.edu.in). Faculty guide: Dr. Archana K S (archanak1@srmist.edu.in).
